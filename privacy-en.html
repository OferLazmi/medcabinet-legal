Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data We Collect

  • Account: Email via Google/Apple/Phone sign-in (optional)
  • Cabinet items: Medicine names, quantities, expiry dates, notes
  • Barcode scans: Sent to server for catalog lookup (not stored)
  • Camera: Used only for barcode scanning (no images saved)
  • Notifications: Local reminders for expiry dates
  • Error logs: Crash reports without personal data
  • Analytics: General usage (screens viewed, actions) via Firebase - no medication names or health data

How We Use Data

  • Provide and improve the app
  • Sync data across devices (signed-in accounts)
  • Send expiry reminders
  • Fix bugs and improve performance

We never:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Allow access to your data without your permission

Data Storage

  • Guest users: Data stored only on your device
  • Signed-in users: Data stored in Supabase (encrypted, isolated to your account)

Your Rights

  • Access & edit: View and edit all your data in the app
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account permanently, or request deletion
  • Export: Export your item list from the app

Service Providers

  • Supabase: Data storage and authentication
  • Firebase Analytics: Usage analytics (if enabled)
  • Google/Apple OAuth: Authentication services
