Delete Account - MedCabinet

🗑️

This page explains how to delete your MedCabinet account and all associated data.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and will erase all your data.

What will be deleted?

  • All your cabinet items (medicines and health products)
  • Reminders and expiry dates on the device
  • Barcode matches saved for you (barcode_suggestion)
  • Error reports and logs (app_error)
  • Recovery code hashes (recovery_code) and device reconnect links (device_link)
  • Your anonymous authentication account on the server

Sign-in is anonymous only — there is no Google, Apple, phone, SMS, or email account to remove.

How to delete your account

In the app (recommended):

  1. Open the MedCabinet app
  2. Go to Settings (leftmost tab)
  3. Scroll down to "Delete account permanently"
  4. Confirm the action

Your account and data will be deleted immediately and completely.
